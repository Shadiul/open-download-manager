## What's new in 0.4.0

- **The browser extension comes with the app.** The app finds Edge, Chrome, Brave, Vivaldi and Opera, shows whether the extension is in each one, and its **Add** button walks you through adding it. The extension now updates with the app.
- **After a download:** open the file, show it in its folder, or extract ZIP, 7z, RAR and TAR archives (optionally sending the archive to the Recycle Bin).
- **When all downloads finish:** sleep, hibernate, shut down, restart, sign out, lock or close the app, after a countdown you can cancel. Also in the status bar and the tray menu.
- **Appearance:** pick the accent (Windows', a preset or any colour), the window material (Mica, Mica Alt, Acrylic or solid) and how rows show progress, with a live preview. Two new styles draw progress in the Progress column: **Ring** and **Column bar**. Settings now keep their scroll position when you change something.
- **A new app icon**, with a choice of **Orbit** or **Smoke** in Settings → Appearance. It also changes the taskbar, tray and shortcuts.
- **A redesigned welcome** on first start: one choice per screen, each with its own illustration.
- **A Windows 11 tray flyout:** click the tray icon for speed, progress and your unfinished downloads, a field to paste a link, and Pause all or Resume all. Right-click opens a native menu.
- **A redesigned Download complete window:** the file as a card you can open or drag, its folder and source link, the time taken and average speed, and a ⋯ menu (Open with, copy the path or link, download again, delete). Failed downloads show the reason and Retry.
- **A branded, shorter installer:** one options page, and **Set up the browser extension** on the Finish page.
- **Fixes:** status colours follow Windows switching between light and dark; dialogs follow the app's Light or Dark choice; tray menu labels are no longer cut off; title-bar, tray and toolbar icons are sharp at every scale.
